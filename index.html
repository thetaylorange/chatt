Our brand colors

Locomotive and Gold are our two primary brand colors. Teal, Blue, Brick, and Green round out the palette as complementary secondary brand colors. Each base includes different tints or shades, with a Pantone Color Bridge reference. We are open to adjusting the tints/shades to accomplish a successful web palette, working around/maintaining the base 6 colors.
